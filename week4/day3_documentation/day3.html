Internship Report – Week 4, Day 3

Solar Dryer Fabrication and Assembly Documentation

← Back to Portfolio

Objective

To cut the polycarbonate sheet and fabricate the outer dome structure of the solar dryer using welding and workshop tools under expert guidance. This activity focused on converting the CAD design into a real-world structure suitable for mounting trays and protective covers.

Activities Performed

  • Verified all dimensions with the CAD model before fabrication.
  • Marked GI rods according to design measurements.
  • Cut the polycarbonate sheet to match dome dimensions.
  • Smoothed sheet edges to ensure safe handling.
  • Prepared GI rods for fabrication and assembly.
  • Cut rods using workshop machinery under supervision.
  • Labeled fabricated members for easier assembly.
  • Aligned rods according to dome geometry.
  • Welded joints under expert guidance.
  • Reinforced structural members for strength and durability.
  • Inspected welded joints for quality and alignment.
  • Positioned polycarbonate sheet on the fabricated frame.
  • Drilled holes where required for fixing.
  • Secured the sheet using screws and washers.
  • Checked rigidity, fitment, and overall structure quality.

Outcome

  • Polycarbonate sheet was successfully cut to precise dimensions.
  • GI rods were fabricated and welded into the dome structure.
  • The completed frame matched CAD measurements accurately.
  • The structure was strong enough to support trays and covers.
  • The solar dryer progressed from design stage to physical fabrication.
  • This day marked a major milestone in the development of the solar dryer project.
