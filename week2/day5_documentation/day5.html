Step 1: Arduino Download & Exploration

We began the day by downloading and installing the Arduino IDE. After successful installation, we explored the interface including File, Sketch, Tools, Board Manager, and Serial Monitor options. Soham Kadu introduced us to the Arduino Uno board and explained the purpose of digital pins, analog pins, power pins, and USB connectivity.

To verify the setup, we uploaded the Blink program to the Arduino Uno. The onboard LED blinking confirmed successful communication between the IDE and the microcontroller. We also learned how the Serial Monitor can be used to display sensor readings and debugging information.
