Slide 1 – Starting Our Project Journey

The AI-Enabled Smart Solar Dryer project officially began on this day. This marked the start of transforming an innovative concept into a practical engineering solution capable of reducing post-harvest losses through sustainable solar drying technology.

Slide 2 – How We Started

  • Reviewed the AI-Enabled Smart Solar Dryer concept.
  • Understood challenges related to post-harvest losses.
  • Studied the overall problem statement.
  • Analyzed the proposed solar dryer architecture.
  • Learned about tray systems, dome structure, sensors, and IoT integration.
  • Discussed project goals and expected outcomes.
  • Established the project scope and development strategy.

Slide 3 – First Steps Taken

  • Divided work into Design, Fabrication, and Electronics domains.
  • Started CAD modeling using SolidWorks.
  • Planned tray dimensions and stacking arrangement.
  • Discussed dome structure design and fabrication.
  • Prepared a manufacturing workflow.
  • Created a roadmap for future assembly activities.
  • Mapped the complete project lifecycle.
  • Defined milestones and deliverables.

Slide 4 – Why This Day Matters

This day marked the transition from learning tools such as GitHub, VS Code, and CAD software to actual project implementation. It established the foundation for all future fabrication, electronics integration, testing, and final assembly activities.


Every subsequent task—including tray fabrication, dome welding, polycarbonate sheet installation, sensor integration, and system automation—originated from the planning performed on this day.


This was the day our Smart Solar Dryer project officially began.
